Apollo is a native Android client for Hermes Agent, the open-source AI agent you run on your own computer. The agent keeps running at home; Apollo is the remote control in your pocket.

Apollo is an independent open-source Android client for Hermes Agent, developed by wutz.io. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nous Research. Based on Hermes Mobile.

Features:
• Live streaming chat with Markdown, code highlighting, tool calls and results
• Answer tool approvals and agent questions in the chat or from a notification
• Browse, search, resume and organize sessions and projects
• Attach images, PDFs and files, or share them into Apollo from any app
• Cron jobs, Kanban boards, webhooks and live logs
• Manage models, providers, profiles, skills, MCP servers, plugins and toolsets
• Usage and token statistics
• Notifications for finished tasks and pending approvals, with inline reply
• Hands-free voice conversation: Apollo listens, sends what you say and speaks the reply while it streams; talk over it to interrupt
• Dictation and read-aloud through your server's speech services
• Layouts for tablets and foldables; Ctrl+Enter sends from a hardware keyboard
• The Apollo design: black and antique gold, classical display type and engraving illustrations; optional light mode, Material You and seven further themes
• English and German, following your system language

Privacy: no ads, no trackers, no telemetry. Apollo connects only to your own Hermes server — ideally over Tailscale with HTTPS — and stores credentials in encrypted storage.
